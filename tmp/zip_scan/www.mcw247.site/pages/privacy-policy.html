Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and safeguard personal information.

Overview

Who controls your data

Our Site acts as the data controller and determines how personal data is collected and used. If you have privacy questions, you can contact our data protection team.

Email: privacy@

Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, email, phone, and address.
  • Identity and proof-of-address documents.
  • Transaction history and payment details.
  • Call recordings for training and security.
  • Activity logs like IP address, pages visited, and device data.
  • Cookies and site preferences.

How we use data

  • Provide services, process bets, and support accounts.
  • Verify identity, prevent fraud, and support responsible gaming.
  • Monitor site performance and improve user experience.
  • Send offers when you have given marketing consent.

Your rights

  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Request a copy of your personal information.
  • Request deletion where legally permitted.
  • Manage marketing preferences at any time.

Sharing, security, and retention

We may share data with trusted service providers for payments, compliance, and support, or where required by law. Information is stored securely using encryption and firewalls and retained only as long as needed for legal or operational reasons.

Third-party services

Our site may link to third-party websites or tools such as analytics providers. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
