Responsible Play

Keep entertainment first

Our Site is built for entertainment. We encourage every player to stay in control and play within personal limits.

  • Play for fun, not as a way to make money.
  • Avoid chasing losses.
  • Set limits and stick to them.
  • Do not let gaming interfere with daily responsibilities.
  • Only play with funds you can afford to lose.
  • Take regular breaks.

Self-check questions

If you answer "yes" to several of these, consider taking action or seeking support:

  • Has gaming affected your work or studies?
  • Has it caused arguments with family or friends?
  • Do you return to win back losses?
  • Have you borrowed money to gamble?
  • Do you see gambling as a source of income?
  • Do you find it difficult to stop or limit play?

What to do

These organizations provide help for gambling-related concerns:

  • Gamblers Anonymous
  • Gambling Therapy
  • GamCare

How we can help

If you are concerned about your gambling, we can help you take a break. Self-exclusion will lock your account for a minimum of 6 months and stop promotional messages. A 7-day cooling-off period is also available.

Contact our Customer Support team to request these options. We also recommend requesting self-exclusion with any other gaming sites where you hold accounts.

Underage gambling

Players must be of legal gambling age in their jurisdiction (18+). It is your responsibility to confirm the legal age requirement where you live before creating an account.

  • Password-protect your computer, phone, or tablet.
  • Do not leave devices unattended when logged in.
  • Keep account details and payment methods away from minors.
  • Avoid saving passwords on shared devices.
  • Use filtering software to block access where needed.
