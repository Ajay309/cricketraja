Anti-Money Laundering Policy

Preventing money laundering and financing of terrorism.

Objective

Commitment to compliance

Our Site is committed to preventing money laundering and terrorist financing and to protecting the integrity of our services.

Core obligations

  • Appoint a Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO).
  • Perform due diligence during registration and ongoing use.
  • Maintain records of transactions and verification documents.
  • Train staff to recognize and report suspicious activity.

Account controls

  • No cash acceptance; funds must come from approved methods.
  • Single account per user, with verified ownership.
  • Users must be 18+ and verified before payouts.
  • Refunds or winnings are returned to the original payment route when possible.

Monitoring and reporting

Large or unusual transactions may be reviewed and reported to relevant authorities as required by law. Accounts may be restricted if false information is detected.
