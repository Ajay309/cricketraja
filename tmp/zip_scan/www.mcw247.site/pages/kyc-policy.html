KYC Policy

Know Your Customer checks to protect players and the platform.

Purpose

Why we verify identity

KYC helps prevent fraud and money laundering. We use a risk-based approach and may request additional documents to confirm identity and location.

Levels of due diligence

  • SDD: simplified checks for low-risk cases.
  • CDD: standard checks for most accounts.
  • EDD: enhanced checks for higher-risk scenarios.

Documents we may request

  • Government-issued photo ID.
  • Selfie holding the ID.
  • Bank statement or utility bill (recent proof of address).

Verification guidelines

  • Name and date of birth must match across documents.
  • ID must be valid and not near expiry.
  • Proof of address should be recent (typically within 3 months).
  • Restricted jurisdictions may not be eligible.

When full KYC is required

Full verification may be triggered by withdrawals, suspicious activity, or regulatory thresholds. Accounts can be restricted until checks are completed.
