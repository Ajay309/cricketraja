Cookies policy

Cookies help the site function properly and improve your experience. They allow the website to recognize returning visitors and keep services working smoothly.

Tracking and analysis

We use cookies to measure site traffic and understand how features are used. This helps us improve performance and service quality.

Functionality cookies

These cookies keep preferences, help speed up navigation, and support services like live chat. We use both session cookies (expire on logout) and persistent cookies (stored longer).

Manage cookies

You can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect certain site features.
